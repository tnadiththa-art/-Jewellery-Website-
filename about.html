BLUSH Stone+ was born from a desire for jewelry that marries the durability of solid gold with the accessibility of modern design. Designed in our studio and forged by master artisans since 2026.

Why We Exist

Born Under Golden Sunlight

For years, the jewelry industry presented two extremes: cheap costume jewelry that turns fingers green within weeks, or exorbitant luxury houses charging 10x-20x markups for solid gold.

We founded BLUSH Stone+ to bridge that gap. By utilizing heavy 18k gold vermeil (2.5 microns of real solid gold electroplated over recycled 925 sterling silver), our pieces maintain their glowing luster through daily wear, beach swims, and celebratory evenings.
