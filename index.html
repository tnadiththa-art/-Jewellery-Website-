Artisan Philosophy

Jewelry Made to be Lived In,
Not Kept in a Box.

Founded in 2026, BLUSH Stone+ began with a clear purpose: fine jewelry should accompany your daily life effortlessly without fading, irritating your skin, or costing thousands in traditional markups.

Every talisman, huggie hoop, and hand-hammered cuff in our studio is sculpted with precision, dipping heavy 18k solid gold over recycled sterling silver cores. Whether worn on sun-drenched beaches, at evening dinner tables, or under your morning shower, our pieces retain their warm luster year after year.

Material Transparency

The BLUSH Stone+ Standard

I

18k Heavy Gold Vermeil

Unlike cheap flash-plated jewelry that wears off after two washes, our vermeil features a generous 2.5 micron layer of 18k solid gold electroplated over pure 925 sterling silver.

II

Anti-Tarnish Nano Shield

Every piece is sealed with an invisible, biocompatible ceramic barrier that repels water, salt air, lotions, and perspiration. Wear it in the ocean and shower with total peace of mind.

III

Sensitive Skin Certified

100% free of nickel, lead, and cadmium. Designed specifically for sensitive ears and delicate skin. Never turns your skin green — guaranteed.
